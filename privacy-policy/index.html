Privacy Policy

This policy details how Battu AI processes data strictly on your device using local machine learning models.

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

100% Local Inference

Executed locally via Google AI Edge LiteRT-LM framework. Your conversations remain on your hardware.

On-Device Vision

Image, text, face, and object recognition are performed offline using Google MLKit.

Zero Account Tracking

No user accounts, server logs, or cloud user profiles exist on our infrastructure.

1. Architectural Overview

Battu AI (battu.ai) is built on an on-device architecture. Artificial intelligence reasoning (powered by Google Gemma 4 E2B model weights) is executed locally on your device hardware through the Google AI Edge LiteRT-LM SDK. Your input prompts, conversation history, and voice inputs are never transmitted to external cloud AI servers for inference.

2. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

The following data types are stored and processed strictly within your smartphone's isolated local storage:

  • Chat History & Memory: User queries, AI assistant responses, and customized personality parameters are retained locally.
  • Audio & Speech Data: Microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO) is converted into text locally. Raw audio recordings are not stored or transmitted.
  • Camera & Photo Inputs: Photos captured via the in-app camera (CAMERA) are processed on-device via Google MLKit models for optical character recognition (OCR), face detection, and object labeling.
  • On-Screen Context: Optional Accessibility (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) and Notification Listener services (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) analyze screen content locally upon explicit user request.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Operations

Network connectivity (INTERNET) is utilized strictly for two distinct functions:

  • Stage 2 Live Web Search: When requested by the user, real-time web search queries are transmitted to web search providers to return current information. Search queries do not contain personal identifiers.
  • Google AdMob Advertising Framework: The application incorporates Google AdMob (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads) to serve advertisements.

Ad Formats & Ad Personalization: Google AdMob serves banner advertisements, full-screen interstitial advertisements, rewarded video advertisements, and native advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, location data, and app telemetry to personalize ads, measure campaign performance, and prevent fraud.

For detailed information on Google's advertising practices, please visit Google Advertising & Privacy Terms and Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID via Android Settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID.

4. Application System Permissions

Permission Purpose
RECORD_AUDIO Converts voice input into text for local AI processing.
CAMERA Captures photos for local computer vision analysis.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Maintains the persistent mascot overlay and status bar notification.
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Stores local AI model files, user drawings, and configurations.
BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE Optional service enabling automated assistant screen interactions.

5. Data Retention & User Control

Because all data resides exclusively on your device, you maintain full authority over data retention. You may erase all application data at any time by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Battu AI > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Battu AI does not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13. Since no server-side user accounts or remote database systems exist, personal data is not collected or stored on external servers.
